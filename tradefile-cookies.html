1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve performance, and provide information to the website owner.

Similar technologies include web beacons, pixel tags, local storage, and session storage. For the purposes of this policy, all such technologies are referred to collectively as "cookies".

Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies — temporary, deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies — stored on your device for a set period or until you delete them
  • First-party cookies — set by tradefile.ie directly
  • Third-party cookies — set by third-party services embedded in our website

2. Legal Basis for Cookies

Under Irish and EU law, we must obtain your prior informed consent before placing any cookies on your device, except for strictly necessary cookies which are exempt from consent requirements.

When you first visit tradefile.ie, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner. You may accept all cookies, reject all non-essential cookies, or manage your preferences and accept only specific categories. Your consent is freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous in accordance with Article 7 GDPR and Recital 32. Pre-ticked boxes or implied consent are not used. You may withdraw your consent at any time — see Section 6.

6. Managing and Withdrawing Consent

6.1 Cookie Preference Centre

You can update your cookie preferences at any time using the button below, or by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the footer of any page.

6.2 Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block all cookies, block third-party cookies only, delete existing cookies, or receive alerts before cookies are set. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent some parts of the website from functioning.

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

6.3 Opt-Out from Google Analytics

You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting data about your visits by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Alternatively, your preferences via our Cookie Preference Centre will prevent analytics cookies from being set.

6.4 Withdrawing Consent

Withdrawing consent will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before withdrawal. If you withdraw consent for analytics or functional cookies, those cookies will be deleted from your device.

7. Cookie Duration

Cookies remain on your device for the duration shown in the cookie tables in Section 3. Session cookies are deleted automatically when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain until they expire or until you delete them through your browser settings.

8. Do Not Track

While we respect your privacy preferences and provide the opt-out mechanisms described in Section 6, we do not currently alter our data collection practices specifically in response to DNT signals. We will update this policy if an industry standard emerges.
